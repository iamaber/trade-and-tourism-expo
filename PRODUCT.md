# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary:** International exhibitors (tourism boards, airlines, hotel chains, travel-tech firms, hospitality groups) evaluating whether to book a stall or sponsorship. They browse on laptop and mobile, often forwarded via WhatsApp or email. They need event details, participation requirements, and clear contact paths to commit budget.

**Secondary:** General visitors and cultural attendees (local Bangladeshi public, international students residing in Bangladesh, travel enthusiasts) exploring what's on, when, and how to attend. Heavy mobile usage. They want to discover cultural performances, food stalls, and the general atmosphere.

**Tertiary:** Government officials, embassy representatives (High Commissioners, Ambassadors), media, and investors browsing for credibility signals and partnership opportunities.

## Product Purpose

The International Trade & Tourism Expo 2026 is Bangladesh's inaugural international trade and tourism exhibition, jointly organized by Nagorik Protidin and Bloodman. Held on August 21-22, 2026, at Aloki, Tejgaon, Dhaka (10:00 AM - 10:00 PM), the event positions Bangladesh as a global tourism hub and international connecting platform.

The website must communicate the event's scale and credibility, and drive two core conversions: exhibitor/sponsor inquiries and visitor awareness.

**Key Objectives:**
- Present Bangladesh as an international tourism hub
- Strengthen partnerships and commercial connections among embassies, tourism boards, and international tourism brands
- Engage international students and connect them with the tourism and culture of Bangladesh

**Event Format:** B2B + B2C exhibition combining tourism, culture, and youth engagement.

Success looks like: a prospective exhibitor from Kuala Lumpur or Istanbul can land on any page, understand the event's scope within 10 seconds, and find a clear path to book a stall or request a proposal.

## Brand Personality

**Voice:** Diplomatic but approachable. Embassy-level credibility without stuffiness.

**Three words:** Worldly, Connected, Purposeful.

**Tone principles:**
- Celebrate cultural diversity without exoticism
- Speak business without corporate coldness
- Convey scale without hype
- Youthful energy grounded in professionalism

## Anti-references

- **Cyberpunk / neon-dystopia:** No aggressive neon palettes, glitch effects, dark-tech atmospheres, or dystopian visual language.
- **Generic SaaS landing:** No hero-metric templates (big number + small label + gradient), no identical card grids, no modal-first interactions.
- **Festival chaos:** Not a music-festival site. Avoid loud, crowded, unstructured layouts.
- **Government bureaucracy:** Avoid dry, text-heavy, unstyled official-document aesthetics.

## Design Principles

1. **Discover, don't announce.** The interface should feel like exploration (world maps, cultural imagery, zone discovery) rather than a billboard.
2. **One system, many moods.** A unified design language that shifts temperature per page: warm gold for sponsorship, cool teal for exhibitor tech, balanced navy for the homepage; all within one coherent family.
3. **Scale through detail.** Convey the event's magnitude through deliberate spacing, large type, and rich imagery; not through clutter.
4. **Mobile is not an afterthought.** The majority of general visitors and many exhibitors will browse on mobile. Every section must feel intentional at 375px.
5. **Respect the visitor's time.** Clear hierarchy, scannable structure, and direct paths to action. No restated headings or filler copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout
- Sufficient color contrast for all text
- Keyboard-navigable interactions
- Touch targets >= 44px on mobile
